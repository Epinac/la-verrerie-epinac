The Stay

Sleep where the Glass Masters once slept, under a soaring ceiling.

At the heart of Épinac, in Southern Burgundy, the house is slowly waking up, room by room. Come now, and you'll be among the first to discover it.

Rooms · 2 guest rooms Capacity · 2 guests each Breakfast · included Wi-Fi · fibre Parking · free
Cul de Bouteille

The deep green of hand-blown glass.

A name inherited from the glassmakers themselves: "cul de bouteille", that dark, thick green of hand-blown bottles, imperfect and alive. Under a soaring ceiling, light glides across a creaking Hungarian-point parquet floor, all the way to the original fireplace that has seen a century and a half of winters.

Pierre de Silice

Silica, the base component of glass.

Silica is the base component of glass, the raw material once heated in the glassworks' furnaces before becoming a bottle or flask. This room keeps its pale, mineral tone, under the same soaring ceiling as its neighbour, where morning light falls unobstructed.

To Discover

What we love to recommend.

A selection of places within an hour of the Domaine, to help you plan your visit.

  1. I.

    Château de Sully

    The "Burgundian Fontainebleau": Renaissance architecture, moats, grounds designed by Le Nôtre. 4 km from the Domaine.

  2. II.

    Autun, the Museum Town

    Founded by Emperor Augustus as Augustodunum, 2,000 years of open-air history. 20 minutes away.

  3. III.

    Château de La Rochepot

    A medieval castle with multicoloured glazed-tile roofs, the very image of Burgundy.

  4. IV.

    Beaune, the Vineyards and Grand Crus

    Pommard, Volnay, Meursault: the wine-growing heart of the Côte de Beaune, 30 minutes from the Domaine.

  5. V.

    Hospices de Beaune

    A former hospital founded in 1443, with glazed-tile roofing, famous for its annual wine auction each November.

  6. VI.

    The Route des Grands Crus

    The classic route through Burgundy's climats, from village to village.

  7. VII.

    Cycling through the Vineyards

    The Voie des Vignes, a greenway through the UNESCO-listed climats of the Côte de Beaune. Bike hire available.

  8. VIII.

    Morvan Regional Nature Park

    Lakes, forests and hiking trails, 15 minutes from the Domaine.

  9. IX.

    Augustodunum Show

    A summer sound-and-light show in Autun retracing the history of the ancient town.

  10. X.

    Épinac's Industrial Heritage

    Coal mines, glassworks, and the memory of the mining railway: the very history of the Domaine.
